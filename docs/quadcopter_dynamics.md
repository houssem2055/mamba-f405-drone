# Quadcopter Flight Dynamics & Control Model

## 1. Coordinate Frames
* **Inertial Frame ($\mathcal{F}_I$)**: Fixed to the earth, NED convention (North, East, Down) or NWU (North, West, Up).
* **Body-Fixed Frame ($\mathcal{F}_B$)**: Fixed to the drone's center of mass:
  * $x_B$: Points forward (along drone nose).
  * $y_B$: Points right or left (right-hand rule).
  * $z_B$: Points down (or up).

Euler angles:
* Roll ($\phi$): Rotation around $x_B$
* Pitch ($\theta$): Rotation around $y_B$
* Yaw ($\psi$): Rotation around $z_B$

---

## 2. Rigid Body Equations of Motion (Newton-Euler)

### 2.1 Translational Dynamics
$$m \ddot{\mathbf{r}} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ mg \end{bmatrix} + \mathbf{R}_{B}^I \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ -T_{\text{total}} \end{bmatrix}$$

Where:
* $m$: Total drone mass (kg)
* $\mathbf{r} = [x, y, z]^T$: Position in inertial frame
* $T_{\text{total}} = \sum_{i=1}^{4} T_i$: Total thrust produced by all 4 rotors
* $\mathbf{R}_B^I$: Rotation matrix from body to inertial frame:
$$\mathbf{R}_B^I = \begin{bmatrix}
c\theta c\psi & s\phi s\theta c\psi - c\phi s\psi & c\phi s\theta c\psi + s\phi s\psi \\
c\theta s\psi & s\phi s\theta s\psi + c\phi c\psi & c\phi s\theta s\psi - s\phi c\psi \\
-s\theta & s\phi c\theta & c\phi c\theta
\end{bmatrix}$$

### 2.2 Rotational Dynamics
$$\mathbf{J} \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}} + \boldsymbol{\omega} \times (\mathbf{J} \boldsymbol{\omega}) = \boldsymbol{\tau}$$

Where:
* $\boldsymbol{\omega} = [p, q, r]^T$: Angular velocity vector in body frame (roll rate, pitch rate, yaw rate)
* $\mathbf{J} = \text{diag}(I_{xx}, I_{yy}, I_{zz})$: Inertia tensor (cross terms assumed negligible for symmetric X quad)
* $\boldsymbol{\tau} = [\tau_\phi, \tau_\theta, \tau_\psi]^T$: Control torques generated by rotors

$$\begin{aligned}
\dot{p} &= \frac{I_{yy} - I_{zz}}{I_{xx}} q r + \frac{\tau_\phi}{I_{xx}} \\
\dot{q} &= \frac{I_{zz} - I_{xx}}{I_{yy}} p r + \frac{\tau_\theta}{I_{yy}} \\
\dot{r} &= \frac{I_{xx} - I_{yy}}{I_{zz}} p q + \frac{\tau_\psi}{I_{zz}}
\end{aligned}$$

## 3. Quad-X Motor Mixing Matrix
For a symmetric Quad-X configuration with arm length $L$ and distance $d = L / \sqrt{2}$:
* Motor 1 (Rear-Right, CCW)
* Motor 2 (Front-Right, CW)
* Motor 3 (Rear-Left, CW)
* Motor 4 (Front-Left, CCW)

Torque and thrust relations:
$$T_i = k_t \omega_i^2, \quad Q_i = k_m \omega_i^2$$

The relationship between total thrust & control torques to individual motor thrusts:
$$\begin{bmatrix} T_{\text{total}} \\ \tau_\phi \\ \tau_\theta \\ \tau_\psi \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\
-d & -d & d & d \\
-d & d & -d & d \\
-c_Q & c_Q & c_Q & -c_Q
\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} T_1 \\ T_2 \\ T_3 \\ T_4 \end{bmatrix}$$

Where $c_Q = k_m / k_t$ is the torque-to-thrust ratio.

Inverting this mixer for the flight controller:
$$\begin{aligned}
U_1 (\text{Rear-Right})  &= T_0 - \Delta\phi - \Delta\theta - \Delta\psi \\
U_2 (\text{Front-Right}) &= T_0 - \Delta\phi + \Delta\theta + \Delta\psi \\
U_3 (\text{Rear-Left})   &= T_0 + \Delta\phi - \Delta\theta + \Delta\psi \\
U_4 (\text{Front-Left})  &= T_0 + \Delta\phi + \Delta\theta - \Delta\psi
\end{aligned}$$

## 4. Cascaded Flight Control Architecture

The controller uses a standard two-tier cascaded architecture:

1. **Outer Loop (Attitude / Angle Controller - 250 Hz or 500 Hz)**:
   * Compares desired angle $(\phi_{\text{ref}}, \theta_{\text{ref}})$ with estimated angle $(\hat{\phi}, \hat{\theta})$ from the IMU filter.
   * Computes desired angular rates:
     $$p_{\text{ref}} = K_{p,\text{angle}} (\phi_{\text{ref}} - \hat{\phi})$$
     $$q_{\text{ref}} = K_{p,\text{angle}} (\theta_{\text{ref}} - \hat{\theta})$$

2. **Inner Loop (Angular Rate Controller - 1000 Hz)**:
   * Compares desired rate $(p_{\text{ref}}, q_{\text{ref}}, r_{\text{ref}})$ with filtered gyro rates $(p, q, r)$.
   * High-speed PID with **Derivative-on-Measurement** (eliminates derivative kick), **70 Hz 2nd-order Butterworth D-term LPF**, **Setpoint Feedforward**, **Anti-Windup**, **TPA**, and **Battery Voltage Sag Compensation**:
     $$\Delta\phi = \text{TPA} \cdot V_{\text{comp}} \cdot \left(K_{p,p} e_p - K_{d,p} \frac{d p_{\text{filt}}}{dt} + K_{f,p} \dot{p}_{\text{ref}}\right) + K_{i,p} \int e_p dt$$

## 5. Upgraded Aerospace Subsystems

### 5.1 Singularity-Free Quaternion Mahony AHRS
Instead of integrating Euler angles directly (which suffer gimbal lock near $\theta = \pm 90^\circ$ during aerobatic maneuvers), attitude is represented by unit quaternion $\mathbf{q} = [q_0, q_1, q_2, q_3]^T$:
$$\dot{\mathbf{q}} = \frac{1}{2} \mathbf{q} \otimes \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ \boldsymbol{\omega}_{\text{gyro}} + K_p w(a) (\hat{\mathbf{a}} \times \hat{\mathbf{v}}(\mathbf{q})) + K_i \int w(a) (\hat{\mathbf{a}} \times \hat{\mathbf{v}}(\mathbf{q})) dt \end{bmatrix}$$
where $w(a) = \max(0, 1 - 1.5 |\frac{\|\mathbf{a}\|}{g} - 1|)$ rejects accelerometer distortion during high-G maneuvers or freefall.

### 5.2 Digital Biquad Signal Conditioning
* **Gyro 2nd-Order Butterworth LPF ($f_c = 90\text{ Hz}$, $Q = 1/\sqrt{2}$)**: attenuates high-frequency motor/frame noise before the PID loop.
* **Gyro 2nd-Order Notch Filter ($f_0 = 220\text{ Hz}$, $Q = 2.5$)**: targets dominant propeller harmonic resonance ($>26\text{ dB}$ attenuation at $220\text{ Hz}$).
* Implemented in Transposed Direct Form II (`biquad.c` / `biquad_filter.m`) for single-precision FPU numerical stability.

### 5.3 Airmode Dynamic Mixer Desaturation
If any motor command exceeds $[\text{PWM}_{\text{idle}}, \text{PWM}_{\text{max}}] = [1060, 2000]\ \mu\text{s}$, the collective base thrust is shifted dynamically (and differential demands scaled proportionally only if span $> 940\ \mu\text{s}$), preserving roll/pitch/yaw authority even at $0\%$ or $100\%$ throttle.

### 5.4 Optimal LQR Synthesis & Stability Margins
For linearized hover state $\mathbf{x} = [\phi, p, u_m]^T$ (including $20\text{ ms}$ motor lag $\tau_m$), optimal state feedback $u = -K_{\text{LQR}} \mathbf{x} + \bar{N} \phi_{\text{ref}}$ is synthesized via `lqr_and_bode_analysis.m`, alongside frequency-domain open-loop Bode margin verification ($74.9^\circ$ Phase Margin, $\infty\text{ dB}$ Gain Margin).
